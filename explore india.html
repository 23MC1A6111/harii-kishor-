✦ Plan Smart

Best Time to Visit

❄️
Winter
October – February
Peak tourist season. Perfect for Rajasthan, Goa, and South India. Cool, crisp weather. Festivals like Diwali and Holi.
🌸
Spring
March – April
Flowers bloom in the Himalayas. Holi celebrations. Ideal for hill stations and North India before the heat peaks.
☀️
Summer
May – June
Best for Himalayas, Ladakh, and Kashmir. Hill stations like Shimla and Mussoorie are refreshingly cool.
🌧️
Monsoon
July – September
Lush green landscapes, waterfalls in full flow. Kerala and Northeast India are spectacular. Budget-friendly travel.

Essential Travel Guide

50+
Insider Tips
01

Visa & Entry

India offers e-Visa (eTV) for 169 countries — apply online 4 days before travel. Tourist visas are valid for 1 year with multiple entry. Biometric data may be collected at airports.

02

Getting Around

Indian Railways covers 67,000+ km — book on IRCTC.in. Domestic flights connect all major cities. For heritage zones, hire a local auto-rickshaw or cycle-rickshaw for authentic immersion.

03

Currency & Payments

Indian Rupee (INR). UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe) are widely accepted. Carry cash for rural areas. ATMs are available in all cities and most towns.

04

Cultural Etiquette

Remove shoes before entering temples. Dress modestly at religious sites. Ask before photographing people. Namaste (hands folded) is a respectful universal greeting.

05

Health & Safety

Drink only bottled or purified water. Recommended vaccines: Hepatitis A/B, Typhoid. Travel insurance is strongly advised. Emergency: Police 100, Ambulance 108.

06

Connectivity

Buy a local SIM (Airtel/Jio) at the airport with your passport. 4G is available in most cities. Jio's tourist SIM plans offer 28-day unlimited data at affordable rates.
